# User evaluation — 0.6.0 explainable signals

Export old results, disable the previous extension, install this copy and reopen your tabs. Confirm version 0.6.0 in the popup. Choose whether to join collection from Activity.

Browse normally; do not deliberately open phishing pages or enter credentials on suspicious sites. Keep your browser's built-in security protection enabled.

- **False alarm:** an alert you believe wrongly identifies a legitimate URL. Caution, warning and block alerts can be reported. Secondary review is uncertainty, not a phishing verdict.
- **Missed phishing:** reliable evidence of phishing that was not identified. Do not guess if unsure.
- Report once per URL. Repeated submissions update the latest report, not a new independent case.
- Record your warning mode and whether you deliberately tested known phishing URL strings. Tell the researcher if your feedback is incomplete.

Passive mode displays warnings without redirecting. Normal mode interrupts both High Risk and Very High Risk; Caution remains a warning only. Trust changes the action taken, not the underlying model score.

Reviewed fixed service and login pages stay quiet. The popup explains when a catalogue rule was applied. User-created Google Docs, Drive, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Drawings and Sites pages can show a non-blocking review or caution. High Risk and Very High Risk interrupt navigation in Normal mode, including on user-created content. Links to other sites are checked at their destination. Your first check after installation should include your usual Google search, a Google Docs or Drive page, and a ChatGPT conversation. Fixed service pages should remain quiet, while shared content follows its model risk tier.

Export the full JSON when finished, review it for personal information and send it yourself. Reported URL paths may still contain private details. You may stop collection or erase the local study at any time.

Many unique URLs can belong to the same website. Model runs are not visit counts, and unreported pages are not assumed correct.
